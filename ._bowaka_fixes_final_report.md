## LIVE BEHAVIOR DELTAS (active since the 14:15 MT restart)

1. **Fill polling**: tracked order ids missing from 2+ consecutive bulk polls
   are fetched per-id (max 5/tick, round-robin) — fills can no longer be lost
   to the broker's 100-row list window.
2. **Exits verify cancels terminal**: a cancel-accept is no longer trusted;
   a child that FILLED during the cancel aborts the exit (ledger
   `exit_aborted_child_filled`) instead of double-selling short.
3. **Janitor adopts raced fills** (`janitor_cancel_raced_fill`) and never
   drops a lot whose parent state is unknown.
4. **exit_pending recovery**: crash-stranded lots revert to `filled` (dead
   child ids cleared for re-bracket) at startup + every tick.
5. **Immediate state persist** after every accepted submit and after any
   management pass that mutated positions.
6. **client_order_id idempotency**: every entry sends its link_id; raised /
   id-less submits record `outcome_unresolved` lots that BLOCK ALL NEW
   ENTRIES (`unresolved_order_outcome`) until the resolver adjudicates
   (adopt, or drop after 12 passes). Exits use `{link_id}-EXIT{n}`.
7. **Partial-child closures**: dead OCO children with partial fills book
   `target_hit_partial`/`stop_hit_partial` and re-bracket the remainder
   (qty drift eliminated). Stop-out counters bump only on full stops.
8. **Calendar time stops**: window anchors to the venue's actual close —
   holidays fire nothing, a 13:00 early close fires 12:15–12:55.
9. **`max_current_return_gate` is LIVE** — candidates already up >68.09%
   on the day are now REJECTED (new rejection reason in the streams).
10. **`protection_violation` entry blocking is LIVE** — no new entries while
    any lot sits unprotected.
11. **MTM shadow telemetry**: `shadow_max_unrealized_loss` +
    `shadow_adv_cap` would-block events (never blocking).
12. **`sizing_zero_qty`** replaces the mislabeled `adv_cap` for qty=0.
13. **Builder shrink guard**: an empty/half-size universe build exits 6
    without writing (override: `BOWAKA_UNIVERSE_ALLOW_SHRINK=1`).
14. **Torn-tail-safe event consumption** + truncation reset — enables the
    new `--data-files` rotation.
15. **Heartbeat monitor now scheduled** (was built-but-never-run) and lifts
    its own KILL_NEW.flag on recovery.
16. **Watchdogs** reset their retry budget after healthy runs and leave a
    gave-up flag instead of dying silently.
